🔧 TROUBLESHOOTING QUICK REFERENCE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Problem: Service won't start
  Solution: ssh jetson@<ip> "journalctl -u fsl-jetson -n 50"

  Problem: Results not saving
  Solution: Check /api/user-info endpoint for authentication

  Problem: Slow inference (>30ms)
  Solution: Check GPU temp & throttling with nvidia-smi

  Problem: High memory usage (>500MB)
  Solution: Reduce HIDDEN_SIZE or NUM_LAYERS in model.py

  Problem: CUDA not recognized
  Solution: Verify JetPack 5.1.1+ with CUDA 12.x installed
