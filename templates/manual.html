1. Overview

Senyasalin is an AI-powered, offline Filipino Sign Language (FSL) learning platform. It uses an LSTM neural network with MediaPipe body/hand tracking to recognize sign language gestures in real time through your webcam — no internet connection required after initial setup.

Key Features

  • Real-time gesture recognition using deep learning (LSTM) and MediaPipe landmarks
  • Multiple learning modes — Learn, Select, Activity, and Auto
  • Demonstration videos for each sign across 5 categories
  • Ghost overlay for visual movement guidance
  • Instant feedback with confidence scoring
  • Progress tracking with daily stats, streaks, and exportable reports
  • Fully offline — works without internet once installed

Supported Sign Categories

  • Colors
  • Family
  • Numbers
  • Relationships
  • Survival

2. Getting Started

Creating an Account

  1. Click SIGN UP in the navigation bar.
  2. Enter a username and password, then confirm your password.
  3. Click Create Account. You'll be redirected to the login page.

Logging In

  1. Click LOGIN in the navigation bar.
  2. Enter your username and password.
  3. Click Log In. After logging in, you'll see all learning modes in the navbar.
Tip: The first time you visit each page, an interactive tutorial will appear automatically. You can check "Don't show again" once you're comfortable, or click the ? help button in the navbar to replay it anytime.

System Requirements

  • A device with a webcam (laptop, desktop with external camera, or tablet)
  • A modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox recommended)
  • Allow camera permissions when prompted

3. Learn Mode

The Learn page is your starting point for studying FSL signs. It provides demonstration videos for every supported sign.

How to Use

  1. Navigate to LEARN from the navbar.
  2. Browse the available sign categories.
  3. Click on a sign to play its demonstration video.
  4. Use video controls to pause, replay, or slow down playback.
  5. Study the hand movements carefully before moving on to practice modes.
Tip: Watch each video multiple times. Pay attention to hand shape, orientation, movement direction, and facial expressions — they all contribute to the meaning of the sign.

4. Select & Practice Mode

Choose a specific sign, then practice it in front of your camera. The AI evaluates your gesture and provides instant feedback.

How to Use

  1. Navigate to SELECT from the navbar.
  2. Pick a category and a specific sign you want to practice.
  3. The camera will show your live feed with a ghost overlay displaying the correct pose.
  4. Perform the sign, matching your movements to the ghost landmarks.
  5. The system will display the AI's prediction and a confidence score.

Ghost Overlay

The translucent landmark overlay shows the ideal body and hand positions for the selected sign. Try to align your own pose landmarks with the ghost for the best accuracy.

Note: The ghost overlay is a guide, not a rigid requirement. Natural variation in your body proportions is expected — focus on matching the general shape and movement.

5. Activity Mode

Test your skills in a structured exercise. The system presents signs for you to perform and evaluates your accuracy.

How to Use

  1. Navigate to ACTIVITY from the navbar.
  2. Select a difficulty level (if available).
  3. The system will prompt you with a sign to perform.
  4. Perform the sign within the allotted time.
  5. Receive feedback — your results are saved automatically for progress tracking.
Tip: Start with easier categories (like Numbers) and gradually move to more complex ones (like Survival phrases) as your confidence grows.

6. Auto Recognition Mode

Continuous, real-time sign language recognition. Simply sign naturally and the AI identifies each gesture as you go — no need to select a specific sign first.

How to Use

  1. Navigate to AUTO from the navbar.
  2. Allow camera access if prompted.
  3. Start signing. The AI continuously collects pose data and runs predictions.
  4. Recognized signs and confidence levels appear on screen in real time.

Best Practices

  • Sign at a natural pace — don't rush or go too slowly.
  • Keep your hands visible within the camera frame at all times.
  • Ensure consistent lighting — avoid backlighting or very dim environments.
  • Results above 92% confidence are marked as correct.

7. Results & Progress Tracking

The Results page gives you a comprehensive view of your learning journey.

Summary Dashboard

  • Today's Count — how many signs you've practiced today
  • Last 7 Days — total practice in the past week
  • Current Streak — consecutive days of practice
  • Most Practiced — your most frequently practiced sign

Practice History Table

A scrollable table showing every sign you've practiced, including label, confidence score, and timestamp.

Exporting Your Data

Use the export buttons on the Results page to download your practice history for record-keeping or to share your progress with instructors.

8. Camera & Environment Tips

  • Lighting: Face a light source (e.g., a window or lamp). Avoid having bright light behind you.
  • Background: A plain, uncluttered background helps the AI focus on your body.
  • Distance: Position yourself so your head, torso, and hands are all visible in the frame.
  • Clothing: Wear clothes that contrast with your skin tone for better hand detection.
  • Stability: Keep the camera steady. A tripod or stable surface is ideal.
Important: When the browser asks for camera permission, click Allow. If you accidentally deny it, go to your browser's site settings to re-enable camera access.

9. Troubleshooting

Camera not working

  • Check that your browser has camera permission for this site.
  • Make sure no other application is using the camera at the same time.
  • Try refreshing the page or restarting your browser.

Low recognition accuracy

  • Improve lighting conditions (see Camera Tips above).
  • Make sure your full upper body and hands are in the frame.
  • Review the demonstration video and align your movements more closely.
  • Practice the sign several times — the AI improves its recognition with consistent motions.

Page loads slowly

  • The first load may take a moment while the AI model initializes.
  • Close unused browser tabs to free up system resources.
  • If using a GPU-equipped machine, ensure GPU drivers are up to date for faster inference.

Cannot log in

  • Double-check your username and password for typos.
  • If you've forgotten your password, create a new account.

10. FAQ

Does the app need internet?

No. Senyasalin runs fully offline. All AI models and resources are bundled locally.

What signs are supported?

The system currently recognizes signs across five categories: Colors, Family, Numbers, Relationships, and Survival phrases.

Can I reset the tutorials?

Yes. Click the ? button in the navigation bar. If you want to see the tutorial on every page again, it will re-show for the current page. To reset all "Don't show again" preferences, open your browser's developer console and run: Tutorial.resetAll().

How is my data stored?

All practice results are stored in a local database on the machine running Senyasalin. No data is sent to external servers.

What confidence score should I aim for?

A confidence of 92% or higher is considered a correct recognition in Auto mode. In practice modes, aim for as high a score as possible to solidify your muscle memory.
